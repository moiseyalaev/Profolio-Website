Garden

Gardening

Every season starts indoors, with tomato and eggplant seedlings under grow lights. By summer the beds, mulched with straw and fed by drip line, are turning out onions, tomatoes, sweet and hot peppers, eggplant, cabbage, zucchini and basil. I built Garden-Guru to track what went in and when it should come out.

Kitchen
